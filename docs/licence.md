# Licence

## This website

[GNU Affero General Public License, version 3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).
The full text is in the `LICENSE` file of the
[website's repository](https://github.com/phoebus-84/video-studio-site).

## What that licence does not cover

**The films, their stills and their music are not covered by it.** Every film
shown here, every excerpt, poster frame, caption file and soundtrack keeps its
own credits and its own licence. They are listed with each film under
“Source & credits” on the [films page](./examples) and in the credits file
that accompanies the downloads.

Some of that material may be used only under conditions. One soundtrack, for
example, is CC BY-NC-SA: it may not be used commercially, and the licence of
that soundtrack is not a licence for the film it is in. Read the credits of a
film before reusing any part of it.

The preview media is not in the repository. It is restored from a pinned
release when the site is built ([how](./site-maintenance)).

The fonts are Fraunces and Inter. Their licences are beside the font files in
`docs/public/fonts/`.

## The software

The software itself has two licences, split along the line of its code: the
reusable tools in `packages/` are Apache-2.0, and the studio and everything
else is AGPL-3.0-only.
[LICENSING.md](https://github.com/phoebus-84/sergio/blob/main/LICENSING.md)
in the application's repository says why, and what neither covers: Remotion,
which has its own licence; vendored code; the models and services the tools
reach; and your own library. Nothing in the software's licence licenses a
video made with it.

## The imported reference

The technical reference on this site is a snapshot of the application's own
documentation and carries the application's licence.
